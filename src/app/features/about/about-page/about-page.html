Rai

Calisthenics enthusiast and creator of Treethenics.

Hi! I'm Rai.

I'm 31 years old, and I discovered calisthenics five years ago. Since then, I've always wanted to keep learning, improving and challenging myself.

Why Treethenics?

Ever since I started practicing calisthenics, I've been interested in categorizing exercises. Having a clear understanding of which movements match my current level has helped me make progress and know what to work on next.

That's why I created Treethenics: to organize calisthenics exercises by difficulty and progression, and to make it easier for others to explore the movements that suit their level.

Let's keep moving forward.

I hope Treethenics helps you discover new exercises, understand your progress and find your next challenge.
